## 2. The GCAO Prompting Framework

> **This is the single most important section in this entire toolkit.** Claude is only as good as what you give it. Most people talk to AI like they're typing into Google — and they get Google-level answers. The GCAO framework changes everything.

### Bad Prompt Example

```
"How do I go viral on social media?"
```

**What you get back:** Generic advice about being consistent, using hashtags, posting reels. Stuff you could find in any blog post.

### Great Prompt Example (GCAO)

**G — Goal:** What do you actually want? Not vague. Specific.

**C — Context:** Who are you? What's your situation? What have you tried? What are your constraints?

**A — Action:** What specifically should Claude do with all of this?

**O — Output Format:** How do you want the answer delivered? Bullet points? A table? Three options with one-sentence explanations?

```
Goal: I want to attract small business owners who are already making money but feel
overwhelmed running everything by themselves. I'm not trying to get famous — I want
the right people finding me and reaching out about working together.

Context: I run a coaching business doing about $15K/month. I post on Instagram and
LinkedIn but I get generic engagement — likes from people who will never buy. I've
tried posting tips and motivational content. It hasn't translated to clients. I have
about 45 minutes a day to spend on this.

Action: Identify the specific type of content that attracts buyers rather than
followers for a service-based business. Explain why that content works differently
than content designed for reach.

Output: Give me 3 content angles with a one-sentence explanation of why each one
attracts buyers specifically. No generic advice about consistency or hashtags.
```

### Your GCAO Checklist

- [ ] Write your first GCAO prompt (use a real problem you're facing right now)
- [ ] Compare the output to a "lazy" version of the same question
- [ ] Practice iteration — ask follow-up questions like *"Give me 6 more variations of #2"*
- [ ] Keep talking to the model until you get what you actually want (don't settle on the first response)

## 3. Web Search & Vision

### Web Search

Web search turns Claude from a text generator into a **real-time research and analysis tool**. It pulls live, current information from the internet — so you're getting accurate, up-to-date answers instead of outdated training data.

#### How to Enable Web Search

1. Click the **+** button in the prompt bar
2. Click **Web Search** (it turns blue when active)
3. Keep it on by default for the most accurate responses

#### What to Use Web Search For

- [ ] Researching competitors (their offers, pricing, strategy)
- [ ] Pulling documentation for tools your team uses
- [ ] Current events and market trends
- [ ] Fact-checking and finding up-to-date data
- [ ] Creating blog posts or content with live sources
- [ ] Researching specific people, companies, or tools

**Example prompt:**

```
I run a high-ticket AI business coaching offer targeting small business owners doing
$15-100K/month. Use web search to research [Competitor Name]'s coaching offer, content
strategy, and sales funnel. Identify their main offer and price point, their content
strategy, their sales language and positioning, and any gaps or underserved angles I
could fill.
```

### Vision

Claude can **see and analyse images** — screenshots, photos, documents, designs, anything visual.

#### Vision Use Cases

- [ ] Screenshot your website → get conversion optimisation feedback
- [ ] Upload a thumbnail → get click-through rate improvement suggestions
- [ ] Photo of a whiteboard → have Claude organise and digitise your notes
- [ ] Upload a design mockup → get UX/UI feedback
- [ ] Photo of a document → have Claude extract and summarise the content

#### How to Use Vision

1. Click the **+** button in the prompt bar
2. Select **Add files or photos**
3. Upload your image
4. Write a prompt that tells Claude what you want it to analyze

> **Tip:** Don't just say "give me feedback." Use the GCAO framework even with images.

## 4. Artifacts

### What Are Artifacts?

Artifacts are **interactive, shareable mini-apps** that Claude builds for you from a simple description. You describe what you need, Claude builds it, and you can publish it with a shareable link.

### What You Can Build

- [ ] ROI calculators for your clients
- [ ] Interactive dashboards
- [ ] Lead capture forms
- [ ] Flashcard study tools
- [ ] Data visualisations
- [ ] Prototype apps and websites
- [ ] Pricing calculators
- [ ] Interactive quizzes
- [ ] Project planning tools

### How to Create an Artifact

1. Open a new chat (use **Opus** for best results with artifacts)
2. Describe what you want to build in detail (inputs, outputs, design)
3. Claude opens a new panel and builds it live
4. Test it and request changes if needed
5. **Publish it** → click the share button in the upper right corner → copy the public link

**Example prompt:**

```
Build me an interactive ROI calculator for business owners who are considering AI
automation in their business.

Inputs: Their hourly rate, hours per week spent on manual/repetitive tasks, number
of weeks they work per year.

Outputs: Annual cost of those manual tasks, estimated time saved, estimated annual
savings, and a simple ROI metric if they invested $5K in an automation setup.

Make it clean, professional, and make the value obvious at a glance.
```

### Your Artifacts Checklist

- [ ] Build your first artifact (start with something useful for your business)
- [ ] Test it with real inputs
- [ ] Publish it and grab the shareable link
- [ ] Share it with a client, your audience, or your team

> **Tip:** Artifacts are incredible for lead magnets and client-facing tools. Build a free calculator or assessment tool, publish it, and share the link on social media.

## 5. Custom Instructions & Your AI Operating System

### What Are Custom Instructions?

Custom instructions are a set of preferences and context you give Claude that it reads **every time you start a new conversation**. No more reexplaining yourself. No more generic responses.

### How to Set Custom Instructions

1. Click your **profile picture** (bottom left corner)
2. Go to **Settings**
3. Find the **Personal Preferences** box under General
4. Paste in your custom instructions
5. Hit **Save Changes**

### Build Your AI Operating System

This is the framework that puts you in the **top 1% of AI users**. Fill out 5 sections that teach Claude exactly who you are and how to work with you.

#### Section 1 — Identity: Who You Are

Your name, your business (or job), what you do, who you serve.

- [ ] Your name
- [ ] Your business or role
- [ ] What you do day-to-day
- [ ] Who your clients/audience/team are
- [ ] Your industry and niche

#### Section 2 — Communication Style: How You Want AI to Talk to You

- [ ] Your preferred tone (direct, casual, professional, etc.)
- [ ] Response length preference (short and punchy vs. detailed)
- [ ] Any formatting preferences (bullet points, numbered lists, etc.)
- [ ] How you want Claude to push back or challenge your ideas

#### Section 3 — Goals & Priorities: What You're Focused on Right Now

- [ ] Your top 1-3 business/career goals right now
- [ ] Current projects or initiatives
- [ ] Key metrics you're tracking
- [ ] What "winning" looks like for you this quarter

#### Section 4 — Daily Schedule & Non-Negotiables: Your Time Structure

- [ ] Your typical daily schedule
- [ ] Deep work hours (when you don't want to be disturbed)
- [ ] Meeting days and times
- [ ] Non-negotiable blocks (gym, family, etc.)
- [ ] How many hours per day you dedicate to specific tasks

#### Section 5 — Avoidances: What You Never Want to See

- [ ] Topics or approaches you don't want
- [ ] Types of responses that annoy you (e.g., "too generic," "too long," "too many caveats")
- [ ] Specific phrases or patterns to avoid
- [ ] Any ethical, legal, or brand boundaries

### Your AI Operating System Checklist

- [ ] Fill out all 5 sections (take 15-20 minutes — it's worth it)
- [ ] Paste it into your Claude Custom Instructions
- [ ] Hit Save Changes
- [ ] Test it — start a new chat and ask Claude something about your business
- [ ] Refine it over the next week as you notice gaps

## 6. Projects

### What Are Projects?

Projects are dedicated workspaces inside Claude that have **permanent context**. You create a project, upload documents, set project-specific instructions, and every conversation inside that project starts with Claude already knowing everything it needs to know.

### Why Projects Are Powerful

- Your **personal custom instructions** (AI Operating System) are always running in the background
- Each project adds its **own layer** of context on top
- Claude gets both your personal preferences AND the specific project knowledge
- No more reexplaining context at the start of every conversation

### How to Create a Project

1. Open the **sidebar** (top left)
2. Click **Projects**
3. Hit **New Project**
4. Name it (e.g., "YouTube Content," "Client Onboarding," "Marketing Strategy")
5. Add a description of what the project is for

### Setting Up Your Project

#### Upload Files (Your Knowledge Base)

- [ ] Click **Add Files** inside the project
- [ ] Upload relevant documents: SOPs, brand guidelines, client files, templates, past work, analytics
- [ ] Sources: upload from device, Google Drive, or GitHub
- [ ] You can use up to 100% of the project capacity — load it up

**Ideas for project files:**
- Brand voice guidelines
- Content templates and formulas
- Client info and personas
- SOPs and processes
- Analytics and data exports
- Terms of service / legal docs
- Past proposals or deliverables
- Meeting notes and transcripts
- Competitor research

#### Set Project Instructions

1. Click into the **Instructions** section of your project
2. Write specific instructions that reference your uploaded files
3. Tell Claude how to behave inside this project specifically

**Example project instructions:**

```
When creating any script, outline, or content brief, always reference the uploaded
brand guidelines and intro formula before writing. Match the voice, positioning, and
content rules exactly as described in the documents. Never produce content that
contradicts the brand voice guide.
```

### Project Ideas to Get You Started

- [ ] **Content Creation** — brand guidelines + content formulas + analytics
- [ ] **Client Work** — proposals + SOPs + client info
- [ ] **Business Strategy** — financial data + goals + competitor research
- [ ] **Team Management** — org chart + meeting notes + project timelines
- [ ] **Personal Development** — learning goals + course notes + reading lists

## 7. Skills

### What Are Skills?

Skills are **reusable instruction sets** that teach Claude how to perform a specific task the exact same way every time. Think of them like SOPs for your AI — you train it once, and it handles it from there.

### Skills vs. Projects

|  | Projects | Skills |
|--|----------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Hold your knowledge and context | Execute specific tasks |
| **Think of it as** | The filing cabinet | The instruction manual |
| **Contains** | Documents, files, instructions | Step-by-step processes with examples |
| **Scope** | Workspace-level | Account-wide (works anywhere) |

**They work together:** Projects have the knowledge. Skills have the process.

### How to Create a Skill

1. Open the **sidebar**
2. Click **Customise** (under the search tab)
3. Go to the **Skills** tab
4. Hit the **+** button
5. Select **Create with Claude** (let Claude build the skill for you)

#### The Skill Creation Process

1. Tell Claude what the skill should do
2. Answer its questions about inputs, outputs, and format
3. Review the test output — does it match what you want?
4. Set an **activation command** (e.g., "proposal," "brief," "invoice")
5. Click **Copy to Your Skills** to install it

**Skill ideas for your business:**
- **Client Proposals** — input client name + details → get a formatted, branded proposal
- **Content Briefs** — input a topic → get a full content brief in your format
- **Email Responses** — input the situation → get a drafted response in your voice
- **Weekly Reports** — input your metrics → get a formatted report
- **Meeting Summaries** — input notes → get a structured summary with action items
- **Social Media Posts** — input an idea → get a polished post in your brand voice
- **Invoice Generation** — input client + services → get a professional invoice
- **Client Onboarding Emails** — input new client info → get a welcome sequence

### Your Skills Checklist

- [ ] Identify 3 tasks you do repeatedly in your business
- [ ] Create your first skill using "Create with Claude"
- [ ] Test it with real data
- [ ] Set an activation command so you can invoke it instantly
- [ ] Install it to your account
- [ ] Build 2 more skills this week

## 8. Connectors

### What Are Connectors?

Connectors let Claude talk directly to your other tools — Google Calendar, Gmail, Google Drive, Slack, and a growing list of others.

### How to Connect a Tool

1. Open the **sidebar**
2. Click **Customize**
3. Go to the **Connectors** tab
4. Find the tool you want to connect
5. Click **Connect** and authorize access
6. Set your approval preferences

#### Setting Approval Preferences

For each connected tool, you choose how Claude handles different actions:

| Setting | Behavior | Best For |
|---------|----------|----------|
| **Always Allow** | Claude acts automatically | Read-only actions (listing events) |
| **Needs Approval** | Claude asks before acting | Creating, editing, or updating |
| **Blocked** | Claude can never perform this | Deleting |

**Recommended setup:**
- Listing/reading → Always Allow
- Creating/updating → Needs Approval
- Deleting → Blocked or Needs Approval

### Priority Connectors to Set Up

- [ ] **Google Calendar** — check schedule, plan your week, find availability
- [ ] **Gmail** — draft responses, find emails, summarize threads
- [ ] **Google Drive** — find documents, pull information from files
- [ ] **Slack** — check messages, summarize channels, draft responses

### Your Connectors Checklist

- [ ] Connect at least 2 tools to start
- [ ] Set appropriate approval levels for each action
- [ ] Test it — ask Claude "What events do I have this week?"
- [ ] Try combining connectors — "Check my calendar for tomorrow and draft a prep email for my 10am meeting"

## 9. The Full Stack: How It All Works Together

### The Claude Stack (Bottom to Top)

**Layer 1: Custom Instructions (Your AI Operating System)**
Who you are, how you work, your goals, your schedule, your preferences. Claude reads this every single time.

**Layer 2: Projects (Your Workspaces)**
Dedicated containers for each area of your business or life. Each with its own knowledge base and instructions — layered on top of your personal ones.

**Layer 3: Skills (Your Automated Processes)**
Repeatable tasks that Claude runs the same way every time. Content briefs, proposals, reports, emails — trained once, used forever.

**Layer 4: Connectors (Your Real Tools)**
Claude plugged into your calendar, email, drive, Slack. Not just thinking — actually doing.
